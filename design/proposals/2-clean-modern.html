🍰

Sernik krakowski

Cakes · serves 12 · 1 h 30 min

ChristmasBaked

Ingredients

  • 1 kg twaróg tłusty (full-fat quark)
  • 250 g masło
  • 1 szklanka cukru
  • 6 jajek
  • 2 łyżki mąki ziemniaczanej
  • 1 łyżeczka aromatu waniliowego
  • 1 szczypta soli
  • 2 opak. budyniu waniliowego

Steps

  1. Cream the butter with sugar until fluffy, then add the yolks one by one.
  2. Mix in the quark, potato flour and vanilla until smooth.
  3. Whisk the whites with a pinch of salt and fold in gently.
  4. Bake at 170 °C for 70 minutes. Cool in the oven with the door ajar.
